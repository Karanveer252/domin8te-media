01 · Who we are

Who is responsible.

This site, domin8temedia.com, is run by Domin8te Media. We run websites, Google listings, social media and ads for independent restaurants and cafés. When this policy says “we” or “us”, it means Domin8te Media.

For anything about your information, email karanhelps@domin8temedia.com. That address reaches the person responsible for privacy at Domin8te Media.

02 · What we collect

What we collect, and when.

When you send a form

The free checkup form on our marketing page and the form on our homepage ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your restaurant or café name
  • An email address or phone number
  • Optional: your website or Google listing link, what needs fixing first, and your biggest headache (a free-text box)
  • On the homepage only: which problem you picked from a short list

The form also sends, behind the scenes, where you came from: the page you landed on, the site that sent you (if any), and the tracking tags in the link you clicked. Those tags are the campaign labels in an ad link (called UTM tags) and the click IDs that Facebook and Google add to ad links (fbclid, gclid, gbraid, wbraid). We use these only to see which ad or post brought you. The form has a hidden anti-spam field that people never see. If our form system shares a scrambled (hashed) contact detail with Meta to measure ads, it cannot be read back as the original.

When you email us or book a call

If you email us, we receive whatever you put in the message. If you book a call through the calendar on our marketing page, Calendly collects the details you enter there (such as your name, email and the time you pick) and shares them with us so we can hold the call. The same campaign labels from your ad link are passed to the calendar.

When you use the client dashboard

Clients sign in to a private dashboard. To run it we keep your account details (name, email, sign-in records), the messages you send us, your approvals and notes, and the business information you give us so we can do the work, such as hours, menus, photos and brand details. If you pay us by card, we may use a payment provider such as Stripe to handle it. We do not see or store your full card number.

When you simply visit

Our public pages use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta, the company behind Facebook and Instagram. It tells Meta which page you visited, and whether you sent the checkup form or booked a call, so we can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. Meta sets cookies to do this (such as _fbp, and _fbc when you arrive from a Facebook ad) and can connect the visit to a Facebook or Instagram account you are signed in to, under Meta’s own privacy policy. The client dashboard does not use the Pixel.

Apart from the Meta Pixel, we do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics tool on this site. Our host keeps basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site running and secure. Your browser also keeps a few small notes for the length of your visit only, such as the ad link you came from and, on the Le Cookie demo, the cookie you designed. Those stay in your browser.

03 · How we use it

Why we use it, and nothing more.

  • To reply to your checkup request or message and send the checkup you asked for.
  • To hold the call you booked.
  • To deliver the work for clients: running your website, listing, posts and ads, keeping your dashboard up to date, sending service emails about your account, and billing.
  • To measure our own ads: which ads and posts bring people to the site and lead to a form or a booked call.
  • To keep the site and the dashboard secure and working.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell your information, and we do not rent or trade it to anyone.

04 · Email and consent

Messages only with your consent.

Sending us the form means you have asked us to reply to that request, and we will. We will not add you to a newsletter or send you marketing messages unless you agree to it.

If we ever send marketing email, it will say who it is from, give our contact details and include a simple way to unsubscribe. If you ask us to stop, we stop. Clients also get service emails about their account and work, which are part of the service.

05 · Who we share with

The services we rely on.

We use a small number of providers to run the business. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Hostinger, our web host. It serves this site, keeps server logs and delivers form messages to our inbox.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), which runs the Meta Pixel on our public pages.
  • Calendly, which runs the booking calendar on our marketing page.
  • Clerk, which handles sign-in for the client dashboard and sends sign-in emails.
  • Supabase, which stores client dashboard data.
  • We may use providers such as Stripe for payments and Resend for service emails from the client dashboard.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver fonts and code to the client dashboard and to the Le Cookie demo page. They receive your IP address when your browser loads them.
  • Vercel, which hosts some of the client websites you can open in a live window on our marketing page. It only loads if you tap to open one.
  • The platforms you ask us to work on for you, such as Google, Meta and your website platform, under your own accounts.

Some of these providers store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local law. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

06 · How long we keep it

How long we keep it.

  • We keep enquiries only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them on request.
  • We keep client information while we work together and for a reasonable time after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • We keep billing and tax records for as long as the law requires us to keep business records.
  • Our providers, such as Hostinger, Meta and Calendly, keep their own data under their own retention settings.
07 · Your choices

Seeing it, changing it, deleting it.

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent to messages at any time. Email karanhelps@domin8temedia.com and we aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days. We may need to confirm it’s you first.

How to delete your data

  1. Email karanhelps@domin8temedia.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. Include the name and the email or phone number you used with us.
  3. We delete your information and reply to confirm when it’s done.

We keep billing records the law requires us to keep, even after a delete request.

To limit ad tracking, you can turn off ads based on your activity in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, or block third-party cookies in your browser. The site still works if you do.

If you are in Canada and not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We protect information with access controls and secure connections, and only use what the work needs. Our site and services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16; if you think a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
