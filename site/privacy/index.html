Privacy

Privacy policy in plain words

What we collect when you visit domin8temedia.com, send us the form or use your dashboard, what we do with it, and how to ask us to change or delete it. Last updated October 7, 2026.

01 · Who we are

Who is responsible.

This site is run by Domin8te Media, a studio that runs websites, Google listings, social media and ads for independent restaurants and cafés. When this policy says “we” or “us”, it means Domin8te Media.

For anything about your information, email karanhelps@domin8temedia.com. That address reaches the person responsible for privacy at the studio.

02 · What we collect

What we collect, and when.

When you send the form or email us

The form on our homepage asks for your name, your restaurant or café, an email address or phone number, and optionally your website, what needs fixing first and your biggest challenge. If you email us, we receive whatever you put in the message.

When you use the client dashboard

Clients sign in to a private dashboard. To run it we keep your account details (name, email, sign-in records), the messages you send us, your approvals and notes, and the business information you share with us so we can do the work: hours, menus, photos and similar.

When you simply visit

Our public pages do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Like almost every website, our host records basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site running and secure. If you switch the background on our homepage, your browser remembers that choice locally; it is not sent to us.

03 · How we use it

Why we use it, and nothing more.

  • To reply to you and send the plan you asked for.
  • To deliver the work for clients: running your website, listing, posts and ads, and keeping your dashboard up to date.
  • To keep the site and the dashboard secure and working.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell your information, and we do not rent or trade it to anyone.

04 · Email and consent

Messages only with your consent.

We follow Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL) and, for US contacts, the CAN-SPAM Act. Sending us the form means you have asked us to reply to that enquiry, and we will. We will not add you to a newsletter or send you marketing messages unless you agree to it.

Any commercial email we send says who it is from, gives our contact details and includes a simple way to unsubscribe. If you ask us to stop, we stop.

05 · Who we share with

The services we rely on.

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the business, and they handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Our web host, which serves this site and delivers form messages to our inbox.
  • Our email provider.
  • Clerk, which handles sign-in for the client dashboard.
  • Supabase, which stores dashboard data.
  • The platforms you ask us to work on for you, such as Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and your website platform, under your own accounts.

Some of these providers store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local law. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

06 · Your choices

Keeping it, changing it, deleting it.

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to follow up and to keep a record of what was discussed, and client information for as long as we work together plus the time we are legally required to keep business records. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent to messages at any time. Email karanhelps@domin8temedia.com and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We protect information with access controls and secure connections, and only use what the work needs. If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
