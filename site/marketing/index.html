Questions owners ask

Is the online checkup really free?

Yes. Tell us your restaurant’s name and how to reach you. We check your website, Google listing, reviews, menu and photos and send back what we’d fix first, within one business day. Nothing to sign.

Is it done by AI or a person?

Both. AI tools do the legwork of checking your listings and site. Karan reads every report before it goes out, and a real person does the work if you hire us.

I’m not good with tech. Is that a problem?

No. We do the work. You reply to messages and tap approve on posts when you want to. Everything is in plain English.

How much does it cost?

Plans are monthly and depend on how much you need. After the checkup we’ll give you a clear number before you agree to anything. Ad spend is separate: you pay it straight to Meta and Google.

Is there a contract?

No minimum term. Plans are month to month.

Can you promise more customers?

No one can honestly promise that. We’ll tell you what we’d change and why, and your dashboard shows what we did and what came of it every month.
