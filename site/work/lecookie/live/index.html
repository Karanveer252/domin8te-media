FAQ

How fresh are they?

No stock. Every cookie is baked for the person receiving it and finished for your date.

How do I store them?

Airtight, room temperature. Best within 7 days; fresh up to 6 weeks; freeze up to 6 months.

What do they taste like?

Soft, delicate crumb with a vanilla bean finish. Custom flavours available for commissions.

How big are they?

Usually 3–4 inches. Final size follows the design; your quote reflects size and complexity.

How do I secure a date?

A 30% retainer confirms the order. Balance is due before pickup or delivery.

How far ahead should I book?

At least 2 weeks. Weddings and brand activations book earlier — inquire as soon as you have a date.
