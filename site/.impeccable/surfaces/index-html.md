Job: understand in one screen that a small studio takes the website, ad creative, social posts and Google/search presence off their plate every week, trust it, and send the short form.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground matched to the chip film; white primary type, one muted grey for the second voice; the brand spectrum lives only in the bolt, the mark and the lit state of a delivered service. Variant layers: A pure black, tight optical grotesk, centred statements, pill buttons; B black with 1px grid frames, Geist two-tone headline, pill badge, solid and outline buttons; C dot-grid black, oversized thin uppercase display broken across lines, small body blocks beside the big words, one bold italic accent word.

No eyebrow above the headline. Slim header with wordmark, quiet nav and its contact action. Phones: same type-led screen over the still, button in thumb reach.

FORM: Category standard (canon), premium dark product page; standing exit taken, not on the ordered list. Seed key 00e695d9.
