## Overall Impression

Careful engineering and a genuinely good rising-line idea, but the visible layer says "AI software vendor", not "a person who fixes your menu, Google hours and posts every week." Biggest opportunity: say that on the first screen in type strong enough to carry it; keep the line, rebuild the rest around it.
