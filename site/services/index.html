01 · Why one partner

Four jobs that should say the same thing.

A hungry stranger meets your restaurant in pieces. An ad in their feed, your listing on Google, a post a friend shared, then your website on a phone outside the door. When each piece is run by someone different, or by nobody, they drift apart. The hours on Google say one thing, the site says another, and the ad is still selling last month’s special.

We take on all four and keep them in step. Change a dish and the website, the Google listing and next week’s posts change with it. Confirm new hours once and Google, Apple Maps and your site follow.

You can start with one. Most owners start with whatever is hurting most this week, then hand over the rest once they see how it runs.

03 · The dashboard

Everything in one place, without the meetings.

Every client gets a private dashboard. Next week’s posts arrive there written and designed, ready to approve or send back with a note. It shows bookings, calls, directions and visits and where each one came from, with a summary in plain words every month. You can see every project, the step it is on, and anything waiting for you.

And when you want to say something, you message the people doing the work, like a text. Every message is kept. You can try the demo dashboard to see it with sample figures.

Questions owners ask

Do you only work with restaurants and cafés?

Yes. Independent restaurants, cafés, coffee shops and bakeries. That focus is the point: menus, hours, specials and near me searches, not campaigns built for a chain or a software company.

Do I have to take all four services?

No. You can start with the one that is hurting most, whether that is the website, Google, social or ads. Many owners add the others later, because the four work better when they are kept in step.

How fast do I get a plan?

Send the form on the homepage or email karanhelps@domin8temedia.com. You get a plain English plan back within one business day: what to fix first, and why.

Who owns my accounts?

You do. You give us access to your Google listing, social accounts and ad account, and they stay yours. Your ad budget is spent from your own ad account.

Where do you work?

We work with independent restaurants and cafés across Canada and the US. Everything runs remotely, through your dashboard and messages.
