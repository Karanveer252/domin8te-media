One more living-flow design, made from 18G Dot Grid: everything is 18G's, and Settings now lets the client choose the ground.

**The choice.** In Settings, the Appearance card has two new options right above Light and Dark, drawn with the same cards: Scenes and Static. Static is the default and is 18G exactly: the website's dot grid on every page, the dots that part round the pointer, and every page reading as 18F's Work does. Scenes is 18F Skylight exactly: the seven skies whose hour and weather change with the page, light words over the deep skies with frosted cards, and the Home sky switch back in the glass dock. The two are never mixed: 18G's own rules apply only on Static, and on Scenes the dots stop and their canvas goes away.

**Remembered.** The choice is kept like the theme, for this version on this device, and both are applied before the first paint, so a client who chose Scenes or Dark never sees the other flash on the next visit. `?scene=scenes` or `?scene=static` in the address sets it for one visit, as `?theme=` does.

**The dots.** They start at 50% visibility, the website's own, and 20% animation, a gentler push than the site's. The two sliders in the glass dock still set them for a preview, with the website's level marked in the middle. The dock shows the dot sliders on Static and the Home sky switch on Scenes.

**Kept from 18G.** Everything else: the fixed liquid panel with one drop for every button, the horizontal team mark, the glass dock with the Liquid glass slider and the Prism rims switch, and the softened rim on the glass that only has the dots behind it.

**Pick from it.** The ground as the client's own choice: a calm dot grid by default, and the skies for anyone who wants the portal to feel alive.

**Honest risk.** Scenes brings back 18F's risk: five of its seven skies are deep, so in the light theme most pages are dark around their light cards. The choice lives on this device only, so a client who signs in somewhere else starts on Static until the preference is stored with the account.

**Dark.** Static after dark is the website itself, warm-white dots on near-black. Scenes after dark is 18F's night: the sea of cloud before dawn, the northern lights over a black ridge, the moon over lavender.
