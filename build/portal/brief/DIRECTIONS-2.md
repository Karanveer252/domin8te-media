## Dark theme (added 24 Sep 16:55)

Every direction has a light and a dark theme. Light is untouched. Dark is the same world at night: ground per direction (11 #171411, 12 #181512, 13 #19150F, 14 #161310, 15 #1B1610), glass at 7% white over the blur with a 14% specular edge, white cards becoming #26211C surfaces with a 10% white hairline, the light pools kept as glow (alpha times .85), ink #F3EEE6 / #C4BBAF / #A39A8E (4.5:1 or better on the brightest glass), accent #FF6A2F with dark ink on it, accent-ink #FFA37E, sparklines #FF8A57, status inks #FFB08F #8CB8F2 #B3B8C2 #8BD49A #9A9E9A on 16% tints, shadows black. Mechanics: `brief/darken.pl` moved every literal colour in the CSS to a `--p-` variable seeded with its light value and appended the dark set under `html[data-theme=dark]` and under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` for `html:not([data-theme=light])`; the toggle (`#themeToggle`, sun and moon) stores `portal.theme`; `?theme=dark|light` forces one. Screenshots: the machine follows the Windows theme, so light shots need `-Query "?theme=light"`.
