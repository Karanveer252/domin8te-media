New ultra-low-noise microphones. 

Using advanced computational audio to remove more noise around you than ever, AirPods Pro 3 let you zone in (or out) when you need to. And five all-new ear tips — including an XXS option — fuse a thin layer of soft foam to the silicone shell to give you even better acoustic performance.

Voice Isolation. 

AirPods Pro 3 reduce background noise and isolate voices during calls. So whether you’re on the bus or in a café, every conversation is loud and clear.15

Adaptive Audio. 

AirPods Pro 3 combine Active Noise Cancellation with next-level Transparency, which makes your voice and the world around you sound even more natural and vibrant, intelligently adapting to your environment to prioritize what you need to hear, when you need to hear it.15

Conversation Awareness. 

AirPods Pro 3 can detect when you’re talking to someone nearby and automatically lower the volume of whatever you’re listening to. When you’re done, the volume goes right back to where you had it.15

Audio performance
The sound of science.

Powered by the Apple-designed H2 chip, AirPods Pro 3 feature a completely redesigned acoustic architecture that brings breathtaking, all-around-you audio to everything you listen to — from your pump-up playlist to your weekly FaceTime call with family.

A new multiport acoustic architecture with more precise airflow control — featuring an inward-facing microphone that points directly into the ear canal — delivers deeper bass, vivid vocal clarity, and a wider soundstage, so you never miss a note.

A custom-built driver and amplifier work with the H2 chip to create high-definition, three-dimensional sound, with superlow distortion during playback.

Personalized listening
For your ears only.

AirPods Pro 3 feature next-generation Adaptive EQ, which customizes a sound signature for your unique ear geometry and fit. And Personalized Volume uses machine learning to understand your listening patterns and adapt to your preferences over time.15

Adaptive EQ uses inward-facing microphones to tune music to your ears in real time. And with new Custom EQ, three intuitive controls let you easily personalize your perfect sound.

Personalized Spatial Audio uses the TrueDepth camera on your iPhone to create a custom profile based on the geometry of your head, delivering deeply immersive sound tailored to you.

Dynamic head tracking makes conversations feel like they’re happening in the same room — whether you’re gaming with friends or hosting a Group FaceTime call with family.4

Fitness
Introducing heart rate sensing.

All-new heart rate sensing helps you track your heart rate and calories burned during workouts.5 Using LEDs that pulse invisible light 256 times per second — and sensor fusion from the accelerometers — AirPods Pro 3 give you incredibly accurate data for up to 50 different workout types in the Fitness app. And with a more secure in-ear fit, you can go all out and they won’t come out.

Fitness app. With just AirPods Pro 3 and iPhone, you can dive into the new workout experience in the Fitness app, which includes things like walking, cycling, and HIIT. You can set your favorite music and podcasts to play automatically when you start a workout. With the new Apple Intelligence–enabled Workout Buddy feature, you can get personalized messages that keep you going.16 And with access to your Move ring — and even the Burn Bar in Fitness+ — you might just never stop.

IP57 dust, sweat, and water resistance.


A first for AirPods, an IP57 rating means AirPods Pro 3 are more durable than ever.10 Thanks to precision engineering combined with robust testing, you can feel confident during even the sweatiest workouts.

Hearing Health
Check, aid, and help protect your hearing.

AirPods Pro 3 bring improvements to the world’s first end-to-end hearing health experience.7 Now you can take a scientifically validated Hearing Test in a broader range of background conditions. The Hearing Aid feature adds improved voice clarity, automatic Conversation Boost,8 and 67 percent more battery life in Transparency on a single charge.9 And active Hearing Protection helps prevent exposure to loud environmental noise.17

Battery life. 

Hearing Aid users now get 67 percent more battery life in Transparency on a single charge. That’s a total of up to 10 hours, making it easier than ever to use this breakthrough capability morning, noon, and night.9

Automatic Conversation Boost. 

Now when you’re having a conversation in a busy restaurant or office, Conversation Boost will automatically amplify the voice of the person you’re talking to while minimizing the distracting noises around you. And with the new microphones, transducer, and amplifier, your voice will sound clearer and more natural than ever.8

Hearing Test and Hearing Aid. 

With a better fit and increased noise cancellation, you can now take a scientifically validated Hearing Test in a broader range of everyday background conditions — from the comfort of your home. And if you have mild to moderate hearing loss, you can use the improved Hearing Aid feature wherever you go.7

Hearing Protection. 

In addition to passive noise reduction from the ear tips, active Hearing Protection helps prevent exposure to loud environmental noise. Machine learning on the H2 chip reduces noise 48,000 times per second while maintaining natural and vibrant sound for whatever you’re listening to.17
