### Hero headline, four options

1. You run the place. / *We run the online side.*  **(pick)**

## Self-edit log

What was cut or changed on the read-back, and why.

- Dropped the old “ad budget, no idea” note. We make the ads; we do not manage spend, so the page never raises it.
- Rejected “We run everything online” for the hero. It promises more than four jobs.
- Replaced “found the moment they get hungry” style lines with things we control and an owner can check: hours right on Google, a menu that opens, reviews answered. Rankings cannot be promised.
- Removed the old “answers everything in ten seconds” website claim. It is a number nobody measured.
- Cut “not a template” from the ads detail. It was a third “not X” construction on the page; two remain (the lede and “not like a chain”).
- Took “every week” out of the social claim in the work section. It was the fourth time; the rhythm still reads through the hero, the f4 panel and f5.
- Cut a filler “actually” and a loose “tie it” from picker option 3.
- Nothing claims photography, replying to comments or ad placement, because none of those are in the service definition.
- AI is not mentioned. No line needed it.
- No fragment triads (“A. B. C.”) anywhere in live copy; the checker counts zero.

## Truth checks for the owner

Each of these lines is true as long as the service works this way. Confirm or tell us to soften it.

- f5 “You don’t have to log in or chase us” assumes the studio gets manager access to the Google listing and social accounts once, at the start.
- Picker option 1 “We build a new one” assumes a rebuild, not a repair, is the usual answer for a weak site.
- The website example promises “quick to load on two bars of signal”. The build has to hold that.
- Social: “in your voice” and “aim them at people nearby” match PRODUCT.md; nothing more specific is claimed.
