In their own words

Questions before you order

How fresh are they?

There is no stock. Every cookie is made for the person receiving it and finished for your date.

How do I store them?

Airtight, at room temperature. They are best within seven days, stay fresh for up to six weeks, and can be frozen for six months.

What do they taste like?

A soft, delicate crumb with a vanilla bean finish. Bespoke flavours are available for custom commissions.

How big are they?

Usually three to four inches across. The final size varies with the design, and your quote reflects its size and complexity.

How do I secure a date?

A 30% retainer confirms every order. The balance is settled before your order is released for pickup or delivery.

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks at the least. Wedding season and brand activations fill earlier, so send an inquiry as soon as you have a date.
