Independents, not chains.

We work with single-location restaurants, cafés, coffee shops and bakeries. The kind of place where the owner is also the buyer, the scheduler and the person who fixes the dish machine, and the online side is whatever is left at the end of a fourteen-hour day.

That is usually when it slips. The menu online goes out of date. Nobody posts for weeks. The hours on Google are wrong over the long weekend. The ad money gets spent without anyone knowing what it bought. None of it is hard on its own. There is just no time for it next to running the place.

Four jobs, handed back finished.

  • WebsiteMenu, hours, directions and booking up top, quick to load on two bars of signal. No PDF menus.
  • Google and SEOAn accurate Google Business Profile, every review answered, and a site written around what locals search for.
  • Social mediaPosts every week, built from the specials board and what is new in the window.
  • AdsOne clear offer per ad, run on Facebook, Instagram and Google from your own ad account.

All four run from one private dashboard, where you approve next week’s posts, see where bookings and calls came from, and message us like a text.

Honest before impressive.

We are a small studio, and we say so. You deal with the people doing the work, not an account manager passing notes along.

We do not promise rankings, follower counts or a number of new covers. Nobody honest can. What we promise is the work itself: a site that answers every question fast, a listing that is right, posts that go out every week and ads that make sense, all kept in step with each other.

The examples on our homepage are made in-house and labelled that way. When we show a client’s work, like Freddy’s Fanpage or Le cookie, we say what stage it is at.

Domin8te, with an 8.

It is spelled Domin8te Media, with an 8 where you might expect an “a”. If you searched for Dominate Media and landed here, you are in the right place.

We work with independent restaurants and cafés across Canada and the US, remotely, through the dashboard and messages. Email us any time at karanhelps@domin8temedia.com, or tell us what’s slipping and get a plain English plan back within one business day.
