Why should four jobs say the same thing?

Domin8te Media runs four jobs for independent restaurants and cafés: the website, Google and local search, social media and ads. Running all four together keeps your hours, menu and offers the same everywhere a guest looks.

A hungry stranger meets your restaurant in pieces: an ad in their feed, your listing on Google, a post a friend shared, then your website on a phone outside the door. When each piece is run by someone different, or by nobody, they drift apart. The hours on Google say one thing, the site says another, and the ad is still selling last month’s special.

We keep them in step. Change a dish and the website, the Google listing and next week’s posts change with it. Confirm new hours once and Google, Apple Maps and your site follow. You can start with one. Most owners start with whatever is hurting most this week, then hand over the rest once they see how it runs.
