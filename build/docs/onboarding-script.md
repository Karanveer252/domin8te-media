## One-time setup fee

- During onboarding, Karan charges a one-time setup fee (between $150 and $300, final amount still to be decided by Karan).
  It pays for real work: the Meta Pixel and Conversions API hookup, the platform connections, and the first week of live
  data flowing into the client's dashboard.
- Why: it raises perceived value. People treat what they paid for as theirs and what they got free as optional, so a client
  who paid to set up the dashboard is more likely to open it and use it.
- How Karan says it on the call, in plain words: "There's a one-time setup that covers hooking up your tracking and getting
  your first week of real numbers into your dashboard. After that, it's yours."
- Karan says the amount on the call only. It never goes on the website, in ads, in outreach, in emails or anywhere in the
  dashboard or demo.
